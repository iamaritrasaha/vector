VECTOR

Earth, in motion.

VECTOR is an independent real-time planetary situational visualization. It renders high-fidelity satellite trajectories and live commercial aviation state vectors on a mathematically accurate Earth model oriented by UTC GMST.

Designed & developed by Hrik
A Foresight Labs experiment

VECTOR is designed for scientific, educational, and portfolio purposes. Satellite positions are propagated in real time from published orbital element sets. Aircraft vectors represent crowdsourced ADS-B observations and may be delayed or regionalized.
